# Appendix

## Maps

A level-two heading inside back matter comes out as a plain centred subheading.

![](cover.png)

Images are referenced by bare filename and resolved from `images/`, so they
work whether you are building locally or on CI, online or offline. Do not paste
in a full `https://github.com/...` URL — that bakes your account name into your
prose and makes the build need the network to render your own artwork.
